About Us

A publication house for researchers and authors

HAK Publication House supports researchers, faculty members, and students in publishing their work in IEEE, SCI, and Scopus indexed journals and conferences, and in bringing technical books to print with proper ISBN registration.

Our team works across the full publishing lifecycle — manuscript review, formatting, plagiarism screening, submission, and indexing support — so authors can focus on their research and writing while we manage the process.

  • Genuine, verifiable IEEE / SCI / Scopus indexed outlets — no fake or predatory journals.
  • Dedicated support for technical book authors, from manuscript to ISBN.
  • Formatting aligned to each journal's or publisher's submission guidelines.
  • Regular status updates through every stage of the process.
